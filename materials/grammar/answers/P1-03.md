# Gabarito P1-03

**A**
1. I don't speak Dutch.
2. The bus doesn't stop here.
3. We don't have class on Wednesday.
4. My flatmate doesn't clean the kitchen.
5. The Wi-Fi doesn't work in my room.

**B**
6. Do you need a bike? — Yes, I do.
7. Does the rent include electricity? — No, it doesn't.
8. Do your classmates speak English? — Yes, they do.
9. Does the library open on Sundays? / Is the library open on Sundays? — No, it doesn't. / No, it isn't.

**C**
10. What time does the lecture start?
11. Where do you live?
12. How much does the room cost?
13. Which bus do I take to the campus?
14. What do you mean?

**D**
15. Are
16. Does
17. Is
18. Do
19. Are

**E**
20. He **doesn't** like coffee.
21. Does she **live** near here?
22. Why **do** you study AI?
23. I **don't** have a bank account here.

**F:** pessoal. Verifica: auxiliar presente · verbo na forma base depois de *do/does* · *be* sem *do*.

**Pontuação:** A–E = 23 itens. ≥ 21 → ✅ · 17–20 → ☑️ · < 17 → 🔄
