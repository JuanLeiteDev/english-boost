**A**

2. is
3. are
4. aren't / are not
5. isn't / is not
6. are
7. is

**B**

10. Is the campus big? — No, it isn't.
11. Are your flatmates Belgian? — No, they aren't.
12. Am I in the right room? — Yes, you are.

**C**

14. Our
15. Its
16. Its

**D**
18. I'm 22 (years old).
19. **It**'s very expensive in Bruges.
20. She is **an** AI student. / She's a student of AI.
21. **They're** my classmates.
22. … and **her** name is Ana.

**E:** resposta pessoal. Verifica: sujeito sempre presente · idade com *be* · *a/an* antes de profissões · possessivo correto.

**Pontuação:** A–D = 22 itens. ≥ 20 → ✅ · 16–19 → ☑️ · < 16 → 🔄 repetir.
