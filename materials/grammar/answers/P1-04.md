# Gabarito P1-04

**A**
1. I am often tired after class.
2. We sometimes eat Belgian waffles.
3. He is never on time.
4. Do you ever cook?
5. My flatmates don't usually clean the kitchen. (Também se aceita "usually don't clean"; o sentido muda ligeiramente.)
6. The train is rarely late.

**B**
7. I study English every evening. / Every evening, I study English.
8. I always bring my laptop to class.
9. We go to the supermarket twice a week.
10. The professor is never late.
11. How often do you call home?

**C**
12. I **always drink** coffee in the morning.
13. She **is always** busy.
14. … **twice a** week / two times **a** week.
15. I **never** go to parties. / I **don't ever** go to parties.

**D:** pessoal. Verifica a posição do advérbio · *be* + advérbio · expressões no fim da frase.

**Pontuação:** A–C = 15 itens. ≥ 14 → ✅ · 11–13 → ☑️ · < 11 → 🔄
