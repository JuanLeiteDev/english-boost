# P1-02 · Present simple: he / she / it + -s

**Goal:** talk about habits, facts and timetables. · **Level:** A1 · **Time:** 22 min

## 1. Look

> I **study** AI. · She **studies** design. · He **lives** in Bruges. · The bus **goes** to the campus. · My friend **watches** series every night. · The lab **has** 40 computers.

## 2. Rule

| Subject | Verb |
|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | work |
| he / she / it | work**s** |

| Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|
| most verbs: **+s** | works, lives, plays |
| -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: **+es** | watches, fixes, goes, does |
| consonant + y: **-ies** | studies, tries |
| irregular | have → **has** |

## 3. Watch out

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| She work in a bank. | She work**s** in a bank. |
| He studys AI. | He stud**ies** AI. |
| My brother have a car. | My brother **has** a car. |
| Everybody know him. | Everybody know**s** him. |

## 5. Say it

The -s has three sounds: /s/ *works*, /z/ *lives*, /ɪz/ *watches*. Shadow: *She lives in Bruges. She watches series. She works at a café.* No extra vowel at the end: "works", not "workis".
