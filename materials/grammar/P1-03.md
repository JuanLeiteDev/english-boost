# P1-03 · Present simple: negatives & questions (do/does) · short answers (A1)

**Objetivo:** fazer perguntas reais no Erasmus: *Does this bus go to the campus? Do you need a deposit? I don't understand.*
**Tempo:** 22 min · **Gabarito:** `answers/P1-03.md`

## 1. Descobre a regra (2 min)

> I **don't** speak Dutch. · She **doesn't** live here. · **Do** you **like** Belgian food? · **Does** the course **start** in February? · Yes, it **does**. · Where **do** you **live**?

**Pergunta:** depois de *does/doesn't*, o verbo leva -s?

## 2. Explicação (4 min)

| | Negativa | Pergunta | Resposta curta |
|---|---|---|---|
| I/you/we/they | I **don't** work | **Do** you work? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| he/she/it | she **doesn't** work | **Does** she work? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |

- **Regra de ouro:** o *-s* passa para o auxiliar (*does*) e o verbo principal volta à forma base. *She doesn't work**s*** é errado; *She doesn't **work*** é certo.
- **Perguntas com palavra interrogativa:** (Wh-) + do/does + sujeito + verbo → *Where **do** you **live**? What time **does** the class **start**?*
- **Exceção:** o verbo *be* não usa *do*: *~~Do you are~~* → ***Are** you…?* · *~~I don't am~~* → *I'm **not***.
- **Português → inglês:** a entoação não basta para fazer uma pergunta. *~~You like coffee?~~* (aceitável na fala informal, mas evita) → ***Do** you like coffee?*

## 3. Erros típicos

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| She doesn't works. | She doesn't **work**. |
| Does he speaks English? | Does he **speak** English? |
| Where you live? | Where **do** you live? |
| I no understand. | I **don't** understand. |
| Do you are hungry? | **Are** you hungry? |

## 4. Exercícios

### A · Torna negativo
1. I speak Dutch. → ___
2. The bus stops here. → ___
3. We have class on Wednesday. → ___
4. My flatmate cleans the kitchen. → ___
5. The Wi-Fi works in my room. → ___

### B · Faz a pergunta (yes/no) e responde
6. (you / need a bike?) → ___ — Yes, ___.
7. (the rent / include electricity?) → ___ — No, ___.
8. (your classmates / speak English?) → ___ — Yes, ___.
9. (the library / open on Sundays?) → ___ — No, ___.

### C · Perguntas Wh-: ordena as palavras
10. time / what / start / does / the lecture → ___?
11. live / where / do / you → ___?
12. how much / cost / does / the room → ___?
13. bus / which / take / do / I / to the campus → ___?
14. do / mean / what / you → ___?

### D · *Do/does* ou *am/is/are*?
15. ___ you a student here?
16. ___ she have a key?
17. ___ the room furnished?
18. ___ you know where the city hall is?
19. ___ they from Brazil?

### E · Corrige (1 erro por frase)
20. He don't like coffee.
21. Does she lives near here?
22. Why you study AI?
23. I no have a bank account here.

### F · Produção: chegada ao alojamento
Escreve **6 perguntas** que vais fazer ao senhorio no primeiro dia (renda, internet, chaves, lixo, aquecimento, regras). Mistura *Do…? / Does…? / Is…? / Where…? / How…?*

## 5. Pronúncia
- *Do you…?* na fala soa "d'ya": *D'you like it?* · *Does he…?* soa "duzi".
- Perguntas yes/no: a entoação **sobe** no fim ↗. Perguntas Wh-: **desce** ↘.
- **Shadowing:** *Excuse me, does this bus go to the campus? — Yes, it does. / Where do you live? — I live near the station.*
