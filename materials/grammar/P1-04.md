# P1-04 · How often: always, usually, never

**Goal:** say how often you do things, in the right word order. · **Level:** A1 · **Time:** 22 min

## 1. Look

> I **always** have coffee in the morning. · She **is often** tired on Mondays. · We **sometimes** eat out. · He **never** cooks. · I go to the gym **three times a week**.

## 2. Rule

**always** 100% → **usually** → **often** → **sometimes** → **rarely** → **never** 0%

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| **before** the main verb | I **usually** take the bus. |
| **after** *be* | She **is always** late. |
| time expressions at the **end** | I call my parents **every Sunday**. |

- Ask: ***How often** do you go home?*
- Word order: subject + verb + object + place + time → *I study **English** **at home** **every evening**.*

## 3. Watch out

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| I go always to the gym. | I **always go** to the gym. |
| She always is late. | She **is always** late. |
| I study every day English. | I study **English every day**. |
| three times for week | three times **a** week |

## 4. Practice

### A · Put the adverb in the right place
1. I am tired after class. (often) → ___
2. We eat Belgian waffles. (sometimes) → ___
3. He is on time. (never) → ___
4. Do you cook? (ever) → ___
5. My flatmates don't clean the kitchen. (usually) → ___
6. The train is late. (rarely) → ___

### B · Put the words in order
7. English / every evening / study / I → ___
8. always / my laptop / I / bring / to class → ___
9. a week / go / twice / we / to the supermarket → ___
10. late / is / the professor / never → ___
11. how often / you / do / home / call → ___?

### C · Find the mistake (one in each)
12. I drink always coffee in the morning.
13. She always is busy.
14. We have English class two times for week.
15. I don't never go to parties.

## 5. Say it

*usually* = "YOO-zhu-lee" · *often* = "OFF-en" (silent t). Shadow: *I usually get up at seven. I always have coffee, and I never skip breakfast.*
