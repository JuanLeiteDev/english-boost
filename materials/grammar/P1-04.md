# P1-04 · Adverbs of frequency · time expressions · word order (A1)

**Objetivo:** descrever com que frequência fazes as coisas, com a ordem certa: *I usually cycle to class. I'm never late. We have a meeting twice a week.*
**Tempo:** 22 min · **Gabarito:** `answers/P1-04.md`

## 1. Descobre a regra (2 min)

> I **always** have coffee in the morning. · She is **often** tired on Mondays. · We **sometimes** eat out. · He **never** cooks. · I go to the gym **three times a week**. · **Every Friday**, we play football.

**Pergunta:** o advérbio vem antes ou depois do verbo principal? E com o verbo *be*?

## 2. Explicação (4 min)

**Escala:** always (100%) → usually → often → sometimes → rarely / seldom → never (0%)

| Regra | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **Antes** do verbo principal | I **usually** take the bus. |
| **Depois** de *be* | She **is always** late. |
| Depois do auxiliar, antes do verbo principal | I **don't often** go out. · **Do** you **ever** cook? |
| Expressões de tempo: **no fim** (ou no início, para dar ênfase) | I call my parents **every Sunday**. · **Once a month**, we… |

- **Expressões:** once / twice / three times a day/week/month · every day/morning/weekend · on Mondays · at the weekend (BrE) / on the weekend (AmE).
- ***How often…?*** pergunta a frequência: *How often do you go home?*
- **Ordem base do inglês (rígida):** sujeito + verbo + objeto + lugar + tempo → *I study **English** **at home** **every evening**.* Não ponhas nada entre o verbo e o objeto: *~~I study every day English.~~*

## 3. Erros típicos

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| I go always to the gym. | I **always go** to the gym. |
| She always is late. | She **is always** late. |
| I study every day English. | I study **English every day**. |
| I don't never cook. | I **never** cook. / I **don't ever** cook. (sem dupla negativa) |
| I go to the gym three times for week. | three times **a** week |

## 4. Exercícios

### A · Põe o advérbio no lugar certo
1. I am tired after class. (often) → ___
2. We eat Belgian waffles. (sometimes) → ___
3. He is on time. (never) → ___
4. Do you cook? (ever) → ___
5. My flatmates don't clean the kitchen. (usually) → ___
6. The train is late. (rarely) → ___

### B · Ordena as palavras
7. English / every evening / study / I → ___
8. always / my laptop / I / bring / to class → ___
9. a week / go / twice / we / to the supermarket → ___
10. late / is / the professor / never → ___
11. how often / you / do / home / call → ___?

### C · Corrige (1 erro por frase)
12. I drink always coffee in the morning.
13. She always is busy.
14. We have English class two times for week.
15. I don't never go to parties.

### D · Produção: entrevista de *flatmate*
Responde com frases completas e verdadeiras, usando advérbios ou expressões de frequência:
16. How often do you cook?
17. Are you ever noisy at night?
18. How often do you clean?
19. What do you usually do at the weekend?
20. Do you often have friends over?

## 5. Pronúncia
- *usually* /ˈjuːʒuəli/ (americano: "iú-jua-li") · *often* /ˈɔːfən/ (o *t* normalmente não se pronuncia) · *always* /ˈɔːlweɪz/.
- **Shadowing:** *I usually get up at seven. I always have coffee, and I never skip breakfast.*
