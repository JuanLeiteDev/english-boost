# P1-01 · to be · pronouns · possessives

**Goal:** introduce yourself and talk about other people. · **Level:** A1 · **Time:** 22 min

## 1. Look

> I **am** from Portugal. · She **is** my teacher. · We **are** students. · **Is** he Belgian? · **My** name is Juan. · **Their** flat is small.

## 2. Rule

| | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I'm | I'm not | Am I…? |
| you / we / they | you're | you aren't | Are you…? |
| he / she / it | she's | she isn't | Is it…? |

- **Possessives:** my · your · his · her · its · our · their.
- Always use a subject: ***It** is cold.*
- Age uses *be*: *I **am** 21.*

## 3. Watch out

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| I have 20 years. | I'm 20. |
| Is very interesting. | **It**'s very interesting. |
| She is engineer. | She is **an** engineer. |
| Their my friends. | **They're** my friends. |

## 4. Practice

### A · am / is / are (positive or negative)
1. I ___ from Portugal.
2. Kortrijk ___ in Belgium.
3. My classmates ___ from Spain and Italy.
4. You ___ late. The class starts at 9. (negative)
5. It ___ cold today. (negative)
6. We ___ Erasmus students.
7. Howest ___ a university of applied sciences.
8. I ___ hungry. I just ate. (negative)

### B · Make the question and the short answer
9. (you / a student?) → ___ — Yes, ___.
10. (the campus / big?) → ___ — No, ___.
11. (your flatmates / Belgian?) → ___ — No, ___.
12. (I / in the right room?) → ___ — Yes, ___.

### C · my / your / his / her / its / our / their
13. This is Marta. ___ course is Digital Design.
14. Pedro and I share a flat. ___ flat is near the station.
15. Tom has a dog. ___ name is Max.
16. The university has a new lab. ___ lab has 40 GPUs.
17. My friends are from Greece. ___ names are Nikos and Eleni.

### D · Find the mistake (one in each)
18. I have 22 years old.
19. Is very expensive in Bruges.
20. She is student of AI.
21. Their are my classmates.
22. My sister is 19 and his name is Ana.

### Your turn
Write 5 true sentences about you for orientation week. Use one negative, *my*, and *our* or *their*.
