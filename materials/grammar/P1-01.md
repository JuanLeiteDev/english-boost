# P1-01 · Verb *to be* · subject pronouns · possessive adjectives (A1)

**Objetivo:** apresentares-te e falares de outras pessoas sem erros de base: *I'm Juan. My course is AI Engineering. Our campus is in Kortrijk.*
**Tempo:** 22 min · **Gabarito:** `answers/P1-01.md` (só depois de terminares)

## 1. Descobre a regra (2 min)

Lê os exemplos e tenta completar a tabela **antes** de ler a explicação.

> I **am** from Portugal. · She **is** my teacher. · We **are** students. · **Is** he Belgian? · They **aren't** in class. · **My** name is Juan. · **Their** flat is small.

| Pronome | *be* | Possessivo |
|---|---|---|
| I | ? | ? |
| you | are | your |
| he / she / it | ? | his / her / its |
| we | ? | ? |
| they | are | ? |

## 2. Explicação (4 min)

| | Afirmativa | Negativa | Pergunta | Resposta curta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | I'm (I am) | I'm not | Am I…? | Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. |
| you/we/they | you're | you aren't | Are you…? | Yes, we are. / No, they aren't. |
| he/she/it | she's | she isn't | Is it…? | Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. |

- **Possessivos:** my · your · his (dele) · her (dela) · its (de uma coisa ou de um animal) · our · their.
- Em inglês **o sujeito é obrigatório:** *~~Is cold.~~* → ***It** is cold.* · *~~Is my friend.~~* → ***He** is my friend.*
- **Idade e sensações usam *be*:** *I **am** 21* (~~I have 21~~) · *I'm hungry / cold / right / afraid*.
- Nas respostas curtas afirmativas não se contrai: *Yes, I am.* (~~Yes, I'm.~~)

## 3. Erros típicos de falantes de português

| ❌ | ✅ | Porquê |
|---|---|---|
| I have 20 years. | I'm 20 (years old). | A idade usa *be*. |
| Is very interesting. | **It**'s very interesting. | O sujeito é obrigatório. |
| She is engineer. | She is **an** engineer. | Profissões levam *a/an*. |
| His name is Ana. | **Her** name is Ana. | O possessivo concorda com o dono, não com a coisa. |
| Their is my friend. | **They're** my friends. | *their* (deles) ≠ *they're* (they are) ≠ *there* (ali). |

## 4. Exercícios

### A · Completa com *am / is / are* (positivo ou negativo, como indicado)
1. I ___ from Portugal.
2. Kortrijk ___ in Belgium.
3. My classmates ___ from Spain and Italy.
4. You ___ late. The class starts at 9. (negativo)
5. It ___ cold today. (negativo)
6. We ___ Erasmus students.
7. Howest ___ a university of applied sciences.
8. I ___ hungry. I just ate. (negativo)

### B · Faz a pergunta e dá a resposta curta
9. (you / a student?) → ___ — Yes, ___.
10. (the campus / big?) → ___ — No, ___.
11. (your flatmates / Belgian?) → ___ — No, ___.
12. (I / in the right room?) → ___ — Yes, ___.

### C · Possessivos: completa
13. This is Marta. ___ course is Digital Design.
14. Pedro and I share a flat. ___ flat is near the station.
15. Tom has a dog. ___ name is Max.
16. The university has a new lab. ___ lab has 40 GPUs.
17. My friends are from Greece. ___ names are Nikos and Eleni.

### D · Corrige os erros (há 1 erro em cada frase)
18. I have 22 years old.
19. Is very expensive in Bruges.
20. She is student of AI.
21. Their are my classmates.
22. My sister is 19 and his name is Ana.

### E · Produção: escreve 5 frases verdadeiras sobre ti para a orientation week
Usa pelo menos: 1 negativa, 1 possessivo *my*, 1 possessivo *our* ou *their* e a idade.
> Ex.: *I'm not from Belgium. My course is AI Engineering.*

## 5. Pronúncia (2 min)

- **Contrações:** diz sempre *I'm, you're, she's, it's, we're, they're*. Na fala, as formas completas soam enfáticas.
- **/ɪ/ curto em *is, it, his*:** não digas "iis". Compara: *it* /ɪt/ vs *eat* /iːt/.
- **Shadowing:** repete 5 vezes, colado ao modelo — *Hi, I'm Juan. I'm from Portugal. I'm an Erasmus student. My course is AI Engineering.* Ouve a pronúncia em [YouGlish](https://youglish.com/pronounce/i%27m/english/us).
