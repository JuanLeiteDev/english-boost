# P1-05 · Wh- questions: who, what, where…

**Goal:** ask any question, and know *Who called you?* from *Who did you call?* · **Level:** A1–A2 · **Time:** 22 min

## 1. Look

> **Who teaches** this course? — Professor Claes teaches it.
> **Who do** you know in Kortrijk? — I know Marta.
> **What happens** after orientation week? · **What do** you study?

## 2. Rule

**Question words:** what · where · when · who · whose · which · why · how · how much · how many · how often · what time

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| normal question | Wh- + **do/does** + subject + verb | What **do you** study? |
| the question word **is the subject** | Wh- + verb (no *do*) | Who **teaches** this course? |

- Prepositions go at the end: *Where are you **from**? Who do you live **with**?*

## 3. Watch out

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| Who does teach this course? | Who **teaches** this course? |
| From where are you? | Where are you **from**? |
| What means "deposit"? | What **does** "deposit" **mean**? |
| What you think? | What **do** you think? |

## 4. Practice

### A · Choose the question word
1. ___ do you live? — Near the station.
2. ___ does the class start? — At 9:15.
3. ___ is your favourite course? — Machine learning.
4. ___ laptop is this? — It's Marta's.
5. ___ do you go home? — Once a month.
6. ___ does the room cost? — €450 a month.
7. ___ do you study AI? — Because I love data.

### B · Ask about the **bold** part
8. **Pedro** lives with me. → Who ___?
9. I live with **Pedro**. → Who ___?
10. **The 7:30 train** goes to Bruges. → Which train ___?
11. The lecture ends **at noon**. → What time ___?
12. **Something strange** happens when I run the code. → What ___?
13. I need **a bike lock**. → What ___?

### C · Find the mistake (one in each)
14. Who does live in room 12?
15. From where are you?
16. What does happen if I miss the exam?
17. How many people does work in the lab?
18. What you think about Belgium?

### Your turn
Write 8 questions for a classmate at orientation week. Use *Where… from?*, *What kind of…?*, *How long…?*, *Who… with?* and 2 subject questions (*Who teaches…? What happens…?*).

## 5. Say it

Wh- questions go **down** at the end. Fast English: *What do you…* sounds like "whaddaya". Shadow: *Where are you from? What do you study? Who do you live with?*
