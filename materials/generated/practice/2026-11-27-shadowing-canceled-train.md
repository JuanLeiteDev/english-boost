# Shadowing · A canceled train in Kortrijk

*Speaking (shadowing) · challenge (B1) · about 10 min · 14 scored items + shadowing*

## 1. How shadowing works
**Shadowing** means you listen and speak **at the same time**, like a shadow behind the speaker. You copy the speed, the stress (the strong words) and the music of the sentence.

- **Stress:** English makes the important words strong and long: *the TRAIN to GHENT is CANceled.* The small words (*the, to, is*) are short and weak.
- **Weak forms:** small words lose their vowel. *to* sounds like /tə/, *for* like /fər/, *can* like /kən/.
- **Linking:** a word that ends in a consonant joins the next vowel: *get‿on* → "geh-don" (American *t* sounds like a quick *d*).

In the script, **CAPS** = stressed syllable · **‿** = linked words.

## 2. Practice · Listening and sounds
### A · Listen twice first (no reading), then answer
```listen
voices: Announcer=f, Leo=m, Nina=f
Announcer: Attention please. The eight fifteen train to Ghent is canceled. Passengers for Ghent can take the eight forty-five train from platform three.
Leo: Oh no. Did you hear that? Our train's canceled.
Nina: Yeah. But there's another one at eight forty-five. Platform three, I think. No wait, she said platform three, right?
Leo: Right, three. But then we'll be late for the lecture. It starts at nine thirty.
Nina: Not really. The trip's only thirty minutes. We'll get there at a quarter past nine.
Leo: OK, but I still want a coffee. Can you hold my bag for a second?
Nina: Fine, but be quick. If you miss the train, I'm not waiting for you!
Leo: Give me two minutes. I'll be right back.
```
1. Which train is canceled? a) the 8:45 to Ghent b) the 8:15 to Ghent c) the 8:15 to Bruges d) the 9:30 to Ghent
2. Which platform does the new train leave from?
3. Why is Leo worried at first? a) He lost his bag b) He thinks they will be late for class c) He has no ticket d) The café is closed
4. What does Nina think about Leo's worry? a) She agrees: they will be late b) She thinks they will arrive before the lecture c) She wants to take a bus d) She wants to go home

### B · Stress: which word is the strongest?
5. "The train to Ghent is canceled." Which word is NOT stressed? a) train b) Ghent c) canceled d) to
6. "Can you hold my bag for a second?" Which two words are strongest? a) can, you b) hold, bag c) my, for d) you, a
7. "I'm NOT waiting for you!" Why is NOT stressed here? a) It's a mistake b) Nina is making her point strongly c) Negatives are never stressed d) It's a question
8. Which syllable is stressed in "lecture"? a) LEC-ture b) lec-TURE c) both the same d) neither

### C · Weak forms and linking
9. In "late for the lecture", how does *for* usually sound? a) /fɔːr/, long b) /fər/, short c) /foʊr/ like "four" d) it is silent
10. In "Give me two minutes", *Give me* often sounds like: a) "give-ee" b) "gimme" c) "guy-mee" d) "gave me"
11. In "Can you hold my bag?", *can* sounds like: a) /kæn/ (strong) b) /kən/ (weak) c) /kɑːnt/ d) /keɪn/
12. In "I'll be right back", the American *t* in "right" before "back" is: a) a strong /t/ b) almost silent (a short stop) c) a /d/ sound d) a /tʃ/ sound

### D · The -ed ending in "canceled"
13. How does the -ed in "canceled" sound? a) /t/ b) /d/ c) /ɪd/ (an extra syllable) d) /ed/ like "bed"
14. How many syllables does "canceled" have? a) one b) two c) three d) four

### Your turn · Speaking
**Shadow the dialogue (5 min).** Use the script below. Play the audio above and speak **with** it.
1. Listen once and read: *the TRAIN to GHENT is CANceled.*
2. Shadow Leo's lines 3 times. Then Nina's lines 3 times.
3. Record once with the microphone: you say all of Leo's lines.

Key lines:
- *Our TRAIN'S CANceled.*
- *Then we'll be LATE for the LECture.* (*for* → /fər/)
- *Can you HOLD my BAG for a SECond?* (*can* → /kən/)
- *GIMme TWO MINutes. I'll be RIGHT BACK.*
- *If you MISS the TRAIN, I'm NOT WAITing for you!*

**Check yourself:** Are *to, for, can* short? Is *canceled* two syllables (CAN-sld), not three?
