# Group project · An email and a notice

*Reading · my level (A2+/B1) · about 20 min · 22 scored items*

## 1. Before you read
You will read two real-life texts from student life at Howest:
- an **email** from a classmate about a group project;
- a **notice** about study rooms in the library.

**How to read:** first read fast for the main idea (1 minute). Then read again slowly and answer. Be careful: plans change in the email, so check the **final** information.

## 2. Practice · Reading
### A · The email (read it twice, then answer)

> **From:** Sofia Rossi
> **To:** Juan, Lukas, Amira
> **Subject:** AI project: new meeting time + who does what
>
> Hi everyone,
>
> Thanks for the meeting yesterday. Here is a quick summary.
>
> First, the meeting time. We said Tuesday at 2 p.m., but Lukas has a lab class then. So let's meet on Thursday at 3 p.m. instead, in room B204. Please don't be late: the room is free for only two hours.
>
> Now the tasks. Juan, can you collect the data and clean it? Amira will build the first model. I'm going to write the report, and Lukas will make the slides. We present on December 18, not December 11, as I wrote last week. Sorry for the mistake!
>
> One more thing: Professor Claes wants a short plan by Monday. It's only one page, so I can write it, but please send me your ideas before Sunday evening.
>
> If you can't come on Thursday, tell me today, and I'll find another time.
>
> Best,
> Sofia

1. What is the main reason for this email? a) To invite the group to a party b) To change the meeting and share the tasks c) To complain about Lukas d) To ask the professor for help
2. Why can't the group meet on Tuesday?
3. When is the new meeting? a) Tuesday at 2 p.m. b) Thursday at 2 p.m. c) Thursday at 3 p.m. d) Tuesday at 3 p.m.
4. Where is the meeting?
5. What is Juan's task? a) to build the first model b) to collect and clean the data c) to make the slides d) to write the report
6. Who is going to write the report?
7. When does the group present? a) December 11 b) December 18 c) next Monday d) Thursday
8. What does Professor Claes want by Monday?
9. What must the others send to Sofia before Sunday evening?
10. Why does Sofia say "Please don't be late"? a) The professor is coming b) They can use the room for only two hours c) Lukas is always late d) The building closes at 3

### B · The notice (read it, then answer)

> **HOWEST LIBRARY · GROUP STUDY ROOMS**
>
> - Rooms are for groups of **3 to 6** students.
> - Book online, up to **7 days** before. You can book for a maximum of **3 hours** a day.
> - Bring your student card. You need it to open the door.
> - If you are more than **15 minutes** late, the room goes to the next group.
> - No food in the rooms. Drinks with a lid are OK.
> - Please keep your voice down. Other students are working in the rooms next to you.
>
> *Questions? Ask at the front desk (open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.).*

11. Can two students book a group room? a) Yes, always b) No, the minimum is three c) Only on Mondays d) Only for one hour
12. How many days before can you book a room?
13. How long can a group use a room in one day? a) 2 hours b) 3 hours c) 6 hours d) all day
14. Why do you need your student card?
15. What happens if your group is 20 minutes late?
16. Juan wants to bring a sandwich and a coffee in a cup with a lid. What can he bring in? a) both b) only the sandwich c) only the coffee d) nothing
17. "Keep your voice down" means: a) speak quietly b) don't speak at all c) sing d) speak only English
18. Juan has a question on Saturday. Can he ask at the front desk? Why or why not?

### C · Connect the texts (think!)
