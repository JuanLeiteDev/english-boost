# A lost phone in Ghent · Past simple (your weak points)

*Grammar · my level (A2+/B1) · about 20 min · 22 scored items*

## 1. Learn it
**Read first (5 min):** [Perfect English Grammar · Past simple: form](https://www.perfect-english-grammar.com/past-simple.html). Then look at the list of irregular verbs: [Perfect English Grammar · Irregular verbs](https://www.perfect-english-grammar.com/irregular-verbs.html). The notes below follow the first page. It explains *be* first, then the other verbs: positive, negative, questions.

### Why this set?
Today you wrote *he turn out*, *Marta come up with* and *She cames up*. The problem was the past form, not the phrasal verbs. We use the past simple for **finished actions at a finished time** (*yesterday, last night, in 2025, two hours ago*).

### The form
| | Verb *be* | Other verbs |
|---|---|---|
| **Positive** | *I **was** tired. They **were** late.* | regular: verb + **-ed** (*I walked*). Irregular: learn it (*we ate, I went*) |
| **Negative** | *I **wasn't** hungry.* | **didn't + base verb**: *he **didn't cook*** |
| **Question** | ***Were** you late?* | **did + subject + base verb**: ***Did** she **listen**?* |
| **Short answer** | *Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.* | *Yes, she did. / No, he didn't.* |

More examples from student life:
- *I **lost** my phone on the train last Saturday.*
- *We **didn't find** it at the station.*
- ***Did** you **call** the police? Yes, I **did**.*
- *Tom **came up with** a good idea.* Only the verb changes: *come → came*. The small word stays the same.

### Two typical mistakes
- ✗ *I didn't went.* → ✓ *I didn't **go**.* After *did* or *didn't*, use the **base verb**. The past is already in *did*.
- ✗ *She cames.* / ✗ *She wented.* → ✓ *She **came**.* / *She **went**.* No *-s* in the past. Never add *-ed* to an irregular verb.

## 2. Practice · Past simple
### A · Choose the right answer
1. Last Saturday I ___ the train to Ghent with Pedro. a) take b) took c) taked d) takes
2. We ___ at the station at ten. a) arrive b) arrived c) did arrive d) arrives
3. I ___ my phone on the train, but I didn't notice. a) leaved b) left c) leave d) was left
4. Pedro ___ worried. He was calm. a) didn't b) wasn't c) weren't d) didn't was
5. ___ you call your phone from Pedro's phone? a) Were b) Do c) Did d) Was
6. "Did someone answer?" "Yes, a woman ___." a) did b) answered it did c) was d) does
7. She ___ that she found it under a seat. a) say b) sayed c) said d) says
8. In the end, the story ___ to be funny. a) turn out b) turns out c) turned out d) was turn out

### B · Rewrite the sentence (follow the instruction)
9. I went to the lost property office. (make it negative)
10. The woman gave my phone to the train staff. (make a question with *Did*)
11. We were very hungry after the trip. (make it negative)
12. Pedro comes up with a plan. (write it in the past)
13. I find out the office's opening time. (write it in the past)
14. They picked up the phone at 4 p.m. (make a question with *What time*)
15. My phone was in the office. (make a question)

### C · Read the story, then give short answers (Yes, he did. / No, it wasn't…)
*Juan lost his phone on the train to Ghent. Pedro called it, and a woman answered. She was on the same train. She didn't keep the phone: she gave it to the train staff. Juan and Pedro didn't go to the police. They went to the station office at four, and the phone was there. Juan was very happy, and he bought Pedro a waffle.*

16. Did Juan lose his phone on the bus?
17. Did a woman answer the phone?
18. Was the woman on the same train?
19. Did she keep the phone?
20. Did Juan and Pedro go to the police?
21. Was the phone in the station office?
22. Did Juan buy Pedro a waffle?
