# Phrasal verbs & word partners · Social life

*Vocabulary, Listening · easier · 20 min*

## 1. Quick look
**Phrasal verb** = verb + small word. The meaning is new.
- **hang out** = spend free time with friends. *We hang out after class.*
- **sign up** = put your name on a list. **come over** = visit someone's home. **look forward to** = wait for something good.

**Word partners** = words that go together.
- **make** friends, **have** a party, **take** a photo, **do** homework.

## 2. Practice · Vocabulary
### A · Phrasal verbs: fill the gap
1. Do you want to ___ out with us after class? (hang)
2. I want to ___ up for the football club. (sign)
3. Please ___ over to my flat on Friday. (come)
4. I am ___ forward to the trip to Bruges. (looking)

### B · Word partners: choose the right word
5. I want to ___ new friends in Kortrijk. (make / do / take)
6. We ___ a lot of homework in AI class. (make / do / have)
7. Let's ___ a photo of the group. (take / make / do)
8. They ___ a party on Saturday. (have / do / go)

## 3. Practice · Listening
### A · Short dialogue
```listen
Mia: Hi, Jack! Are you free on Saturday?
Jack: Yes, I think so. Why?
Mia: We are having a party at my flat. Can you come over?
Jack: Sure! What time does it start?
Mia: At seven. I'll bring chips. Can you bring drinks?
Jack: No problem. I'm looking forward to it!
```
9. What day is the party?
10. What does Mia bring?
11. What time does the party start?

### B · A class
```listen
Professor: Good morning, everyone. Today we talk about your AI project. Please take notes, because I will not repeat this. You work in groups of three. Each group does some research and makes a short presentation. The project is due next Friday. If you have questions, sign up for my office hour on the door.
```
12. What must students do during the talk?
13. How many students are in a group?
14. When is the project due?

### Your turn · Speaking
Record 1 minute in the chat (microphone): invite a new classmate to a party. Use **come over**, **hang out** and **have a party**.
