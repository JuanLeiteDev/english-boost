# Social life · Phrasal verbs, word partners & listening

*Vocabulary, Listening · easier (A2+) · about 30 min · 30 scored items + speaking*

## 1. Quick look
**Phrasal verb** = verb + small word (*out, up, over, on*…). Together they have a **new meaning**.

| Phrasal verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **hang out** | spend free time together | *We hang out at the student bar.* |
| **come over** | visit someone's home | *Come over on Friday!* |
| **sign up (for)** | put your name on a list | *I signed up for the football team.* |
| **catch up** | talk about your news | *Let's have a coffee and catch up.* |
| **show up** | arrive (often late) | *He showed up at 11.* |
| **look forward to + -ing** | be happy about a future thing | *I'm looking forward to seeing you.* |

**Word partners:** **make** friends / a mistake · **do** homework / sport · **have** a party / dinner · **take** a photo / a break.

## 2. Practice · Phrasal verbs
### A · Read the meaning, write the phrasal verb
1. My friends and I usually ___ at the student bar on Thursdays. (= spend free time together)
2. Do you want to ___ to my flat on Saturday? We can cook together. (= visit my home)
3. I want to ___ for the university football team. Where is the list? (= put my name on it)
4. I haven't seen you since September! Let's have a coffee and ___. (= talk about our news)
5. The party started at 8, but Marco didn't ___ until 10:30. (= arrive)
6. I'm really ___ the trip to Bruges next week! (= happy about it before it happens)

### B · Choose the small word
7. Can you pick me ___ at the station at 7? (up / on / out)
8. I get ___ well with my flatmates. We never argue. (on / up / over)
9. We ran ___ of milk, so I went to the shop. (out / off / up)
10. Please turn the music ___ a little. It's too loud and my neighbor is sleeping. (down / off / out)
11. I'm not ready yet. Can you hold ___ for five minutes? (on / up / out)

## 3. Practice · Word partners
### C · Make, do, have or take? Use the right form
12. It's easy to ___ friends in an Erasmus group. (make / do / have / take)
13. Can you ___ a photo of us in front of the Belfry? (make / do / take / have)
14. We're going to ___ a party for Sara's birthday. (have / do / make / take)
15. I can't go out tonight. I have to ___ my AI homework. (do / make / have / take)
16. Sorry, I ___ a mistake yesterday: the party is on Saturday, not Friday. (make / do / take)
17. We worked for three hours. Let's ___ a break. (take / make / do)

### D · Find the mistake and write the correct sentence
18. Yesterday I made my homework with Pedro.
19. We hanged out at the park after class.
20. I am looking forward to see you on Saturday.
21. She takes a lot of friends at university.

## 4. Practice · Listening
### E · A phone call (listen twice, then answer; open the transcript only at the end)
```listen
voices: Lucas=m, Mia=f
Lucas: Hi Mia, it's Lucas. Listen, about the dinner at my place. We said Friday at seven, right?
Mia: Yes, Friday at seven. Is there a problem?
Lucas: A small one. My flatmate has an exam on Saturday morning, so Friday is not great for him. Can we move it to Saturday? Same time is too early for me, so let's say eight.
Mia: Saturday at eight is fine. Should I bring some drinks?
Lucas: No, no, we have lots of drinks. Can you bring a dessert? Something sweet, maybe a cake.
Mia: Sure. And how many people are coming?
Lucas: Six for sure. Maybe seven, if Tom finishes work early.
Mia: Great. What's the address again?
Lucas: Groeningestraat fourteen. Second floor. Oh, and the bell doesn't work, so just call me when you're outside and I'll come down.
Mia: Perfect. I'm looking forward to it!
```

### Your turn · Speaking
**Role-play.** You call a classmate to invite them to a dinner at your flat (record 1–2 minutes in the chat with the microphone, or write it).
- Say **when** and **where**, and change one detail (the day or the time), like Lucas.
- Use at least **3 phrasal verbs** from this set and **2 word partners** (*make friends, have a party…*).
- Check yourself: did you use *looking forward to + -ing*? Did you say the address clearly?
