## Feedback

- Good: "My mom has 2 jobs." and "She studies during the week and she works on Saturdays." The verbs for *she* are correct.
- "since 2022": You started living here in the past and still live here. Think about *have lived*.
- "presencial to Portugal and remote to Brazil": Use *in person* for work at a place, and *remotely* for work online.
- "more two relatives": Put *two* before *more*.
- "they working": For a usual fact, use the present simple.

Try one rewrite. Keep your ideas and correct these four parts.
