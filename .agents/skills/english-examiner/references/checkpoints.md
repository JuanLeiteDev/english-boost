All papers come from `materials/official/` (fetched with `scripts/fetch-official-materials.sh`). File names are partial: find them with `ls materials/official/*/*/`.
Re-using the same sample paper at two checkpoints inflates the score, so each checkpoint uses different parts.

- Speaking: 4 monologue prompts, via a dictation transcript (13')

## C1: 2027-01-02 · End of Phase 1 · 60 min
- Grammar (15'): 25 new items across P1-01 – P1-28. Write them fresh, weighted towards units that were 🔄 at any point. Put the key in `assessments/answers/C1-key.md` **before** giving the test.
- Listening (15'): **B1 Preliminary Listening Parts 2 and 3**. Official key.
- Reading (15'): **B1 Preliminary Reading Parts 1 and 2**. Official key.
- Writing (10'): **B1 Preliminary Writing Part 1** (email, about 100 words). Rubric: B1 writing.
- Speaking (5'): B1 Preliminary Speaking Part 2 (describe a photo; the learner describes it from the PDF) plus a 2-minute 4/3/2 monologue on "my plans for Belgium". Rubric: B1 speaking.

## C2: 2027-01-16 · Mid Phase 2 · 45 min (short)
- Listening (15'): **B1 Preliminary Listening Part 4**, plus one 3–5 min authentic academic clip from `resources/catalog.md`, with 5 comprehension questions you write after reading its transcript.
- Reading (10'): **B1 Preliminary Reading Part 3**.
- Writing (10'): an email to a professor asking for an extension or clarification (120 words). Rubric: B1 writing, with formal register under Communicative Achievement.
- Speaking (10'): 2 role-plays from `speaking-coach/references/erasmus-scenarios.md` (one admin, one academic), from transcripts.

## C3: 2027-01-31 · Final · 90 min
- Grammar (15'): 30 items covering P1 and P2, key written in advance.
- Listening (20'): **B1 Preliminary Listening Parts 1–4** (full; Part 1 was used at C0 two months earlier, which is acceptable) **plus B2 First Listening Part 1**.
- Reading (20'): **B1 Preliminary Reading Parts 4–5**, plus **B2 First Reading Part 5** (stretch).
- Writing (20'): **B1 Preliminary Writing Part 2** (article or story) **and** a B2-style essay or report from the B2 First Writing paper (stretch). Rubrics: B1 and B2.
- Speaking (15'): a 3-minute presentation of an AI project (prepared), Q&A role-play, and B2 First Speaking Part 2 (compare photos).
- Output: a full comparison of C0 → C1 → C2 → C3, plus a "what to keep doing during the Erasmus" list.
