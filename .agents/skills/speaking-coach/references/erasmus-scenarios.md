# Erasmus scenarios (Howest · Kortrijk/Bruges · Flanders)

Context: Flanders is Dutch-speaking, but English is widely used at Howest and by younger people. Many classmates are non-native speakers, so practise understanding varied accents and checking meaning. Each scenario has a **goal**, a **complication** to introduce mid-way and **target language**. The level column says from when the scenario is reasonable.

## Week 7: Arrival and admin

| # | Scenario | Goal | Complication | Target language | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Picking up keys at the student residence | Get the keys, understand the rules, check the room | The room isn't ready yet / the deposit amount differs from the email | *I booked…, I was told…, Could you explain…, Is it possible to…* | A2+ |
| A2 | Reporting a problem to the landlord | Get the heating or Wi-Fi fixed by a date | The landlord says it's the tenant's fault | *It's been broken since…, Could someone come…, When can I expect…* | B1 |
| A3 | City hall registration (*inschrijving*) | Book or complete the registration and know which documents are needed | A missing document (proof of address / passport copy) | *What do I need to bring…, I don't have… yet, Can I send it later?* | B1 |
| A4 | Opening a bank account / getting a SIM card | Choose an option and understand the costs | Monthly fees that weren't mentioned online | *Are there any fees…, What's the difference between…, I'd rather…* | A2+ |
| A5 | Buying a train ticket / renting a bike | Get the right ticket and understand the rules | A strike or a cancelled train; the station staff answer in Dutch first | *Sorry, do you speak English? Is there another way to get to…?* | A2 |
| A6 | Pharmacy / GP appointment | Describe symptoms and understand the instructions | The doctor asks follow-up questions quickly | *I've had a … since…, It hurts when…, How often should I take…?* | B1 |

## Week 8: Academic life

| # | Scenario | Goal | Complication | Target language | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Introducing yourself in the first class | 30-second intro: name, home university, why AI, expectations | The teacher asks a follow-up you didn't expect | *I'm doing…, I'm especially interested in…* | A2+ |
| B2 | Asking a lecturer to clarify | Understand an assignment requirement | The lecturer re-explains with jargon | *Sorry, could you rephrase…? Do you mean that…? So, if I understand correctly…* | B1 |
| B3 | First group-project meeting | Split the tasks and agree on deadlines and tools | A teammate wants to do everything differently | *How about if…, I'd prefer to…, Shall we…, Let's agree on…* | B1 |
| B4 | Office hours: a grade or an extension | Get feedback or an extension politely | The answer is "no", so negotiate an alternative | *I was wondering if…, Would it be possible…, I understand, but…* | B1+ |
| B5 | Library / IT helpdesk | Fix a login or printing problem | The problem needs a form or another office | *It says…, I've already tried…, Where exactly is…?* | A2+ |

## Week 9: AI and tech English

| # | Scenario | Goal | Complication | Target language | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Explaining your past project to a classmate | Explain what it does, how and the result in 2 minutes | "Why didn't you use X?" | *It's a model that…, We trained it on…, The main challenge was…* | B1 |
| C2 | Code review / pair programming | Explain a bug and propose a fix | Disagreement about the fix | *The issue is that…, What if we…, I see your point, but…* | B1+ |
| C3 | Mini-presentation + Q&A (3') | A clear structure with signposting | An unclear or hostile question | *First…, Moving on to…, That's a good question; I'd say…* | B1+ |
| C4 | Asking for help on a technical forum / Slack | A clear question with context | Someone asks for more details | *I'm trying to…, I expected…, but…, Here's what I've tried…* | B1 |

## Week 10: Social and intercultural

| # | Scenario | Goal | Complication | Target language | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Small talk at the welcome party | Keep a 5-minute conversation going with follow-up questions | Loud music: ask people to repeat | *So, how do you like…? What made you choose…? Oh really, me too!* | A2+ |
| D2 | Making plans with friends | Agree on a day, place and time | A friend cancels and you rearrange | *Are you free on…? How about…? Let's make it…* | A2+ |
| D3 | Sharing an opinion (food, cultures, AI ethics) | Give an opinion with a reason and react to others | A strong disagreement | *In my opinion…, I see it differently…, That's true, but…* | B1 |
| D4 | Flatmate conflict (cleaning, noise) | Solve it politely | The flatmate gets defensive | *I just wanted to talk about…, Would you mind…? How about we…* | B1 |
| D5 | Telling a story (a funny or bad first week) | A 2-minute narrative with past tenses | Questions in the middle of the story | narrative tenses (P2-02), *Anyway…, In the end…* | B1 |

## Phase 1 (lighter versions)
Week 1: B1 (intro) and D1 (small talk, simple) · Week 2: A1 (keys, basic) and A5 (ticket) · Week 3: D5 (simple past story) · Week 4: D2 (plans), A6 (simple symptoms) · Week 5: A4 (bank/SIM), B5 (helpdesk).
