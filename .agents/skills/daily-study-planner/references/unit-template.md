# Grammar unit template

Use `materials/grammar/P1-02.md` as the gold example. Everything is in **simple English** (the learner is around A2).

**The explanation comes from a real page, and it is complete.** Juan's feedback (2026-11-27): short summaries made up by the tutor have too much in them and too little quality. Before you write the Rule, open the page for this unit in the `## Explanations` section of `resources/exercise-links.md` and read it. Link it at the top of the Rule and explain the point from that page: what it means, when we use it, the form, examples, the typical mistake. Quantity is not quality: one point explained well beats five points in a list.

Answer key (`materials/grammar/answers/<ID>.md`): one numbered line per scored item (`1. answer`). Alternatives go after ` / `. Optional words go in `( )`. Notes go in English in `( )`, for example `(subject)` or `(also accepted: …)`. End with `**Score:** N items · …`.

Quality checks:
- Number the items 1..N with no gaps, across sections A–D. Items under "Your turn" are not in the key.
- Every scored item has one clear answer, or the key lists all accepted variants.
- In reorder tasks, the given words must produce the correct answer.
- Use American spelling. Use real contexts: Howest, Kortrijk/Bruges, flatmates, AI coursework, city hall, bank.
